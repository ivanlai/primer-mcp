## Parent Story
[[ST-022]]

## What to do
**What's gone wrong:** `_update_section` matches headings as substrings (tickets.py:62) — it locates a section with `body.index("## " + heading)`, a raw substring search, so a heading that is a prefix of another (or heading-like text mid-line) matches the wrong location and silently corrupts the body. Two corruption paths:

- `update_ticket(ticket_id="TK-001", body_sections={"What": ...})` on a task matches `## What to do`, splices content after `## What`, and silently rewrites the heading — destroying " to do" and the section. No error, permanent corruption.
- A body whose prose mentions `## Findings` mid-paragraph (e.g. quoting another ticket) matches before the real heading, and content gets injected mid-paragraph.

Fix: match headings as whole lines (e.g. anchor on line starts and compare the full heading line). Unknown section names should keep raising the existing steering error.

## Testable Outcome
Updating a section whose name is a prefix of another heading (e.g. "What" vs "## What to do") raises a steering error instead of corrupting the body; a heading-like string mid-line is not treated as a heading.

## Dependencies
- (none)

## Completion Notes
Replaced `body.index(marker)` substring search with `re.search(r"^...marker...$", body, re.MULTILINE)` so headings match as whole lines only. Prefix headings (e.g. "What" vs "What to do") now raise ValueError instead of silently corrupting the body, and heading-like text mid-line in prose is ignored. Added 4 tests covering exact match, prefix rejection, mid-line rejection, and missing heading.
