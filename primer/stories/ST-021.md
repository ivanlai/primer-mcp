## Parent Epic

## What
Replace hard gates with soft nudges. The server advises the planning-first workflow instead of blocking operations that skip steps. The AI infers task completion from conversation context and prompts the user to confirm, rather than requiring explicit ceremony. Gates become suggestions; the two-phase flow is driven by the AI, not remembered by the user.
