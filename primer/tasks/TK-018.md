## Completion Notes
Added two tools: `delete_ticket` (deletes one file, warns about non-todo status and orphaned children) and `sweep_blocked_by` (one-time scan to remove dangling blocked_by references). Design evolved during planning — no hard gates on status or children, just warnings, since git is the safety net and the LLM handles cascading by calling delete_ticket in a loop. Also renamed `query.py` to `store.py` since the module now holds reads, updates, deletes, and sweeps.
