## What to do
**What's gone wrong** — two blind spots in the `list_actionable` output, both in store.py:

1. In-progress spikes are invisible (store.py:422). The "In progress" section filters `isinstance(t, Task)`, so a running spike appears nowhere — not in Urgent (Task-only), not In progress (Task-only), not the Actionable table (todo-only). If the spike is the only open item, the output ends "Nothing outstanding in EP-001, but it is not done — check its stories", hiding the running spike entirely. The same Task-only filter at line 412 has the equivalent effect for completed spikes.

2. Childless todo stories skip the blocked_by check (store.py:435). A story with no tasks yet is listed as actionable ("needs tasks") without going through `_unfinished_blockers`, unlike tasks/spikes (line 448). A story blocked on another open story is therefore offered as ready to break down, contradicting its dependency ordering.

## Testable Outcome
