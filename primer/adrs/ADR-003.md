## Parent Epic
[[EP-001]]

## Context
Two mechanisms could express blocking: the `blocked_by` edge list and the `blocked` status value. Without a rule they overlap, and it is unclear whether a ticket waiting on an unfinished dependency should have its status rewritten. `blocked` is currently unreachable — three tools reject it, start_task accepts it, nothing sets it.

## Decision
Keep them strictly separate. `blocked_by` expresses ticket-to-ticket dependency and readiness is computed from the graph on every get_next_action call — such a ticket keeps `status: todo` and is simply not offered. `status: blocked` is reserved for blockers with no ticket (waiting on credentials, on legal review), which the graph cannot express. update_ticket is the first and only writer of that status.

## Alternatives Considered
- Derive `blocked` from blocked_by and persist it — rejected: every status change would have to recompute the stored status of every dependent, a second cascade fanning outward across the graph, buying nothing because readiness is already computed per call
- Drop the `blocked` status entirely and rely on blocked_by — rejected: external blockers become invisible, and the Jira mapping (blocked -> flagged) assumes it exists

## Consequences
One meaning per mechanism and no outward cascade anywhere in the system. This is what makes the reopen_task story cheap: reopening a task automatically stops its dependents being offered, with zero writes.
