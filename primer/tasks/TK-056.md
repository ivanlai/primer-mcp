## What to do
Found while dogfooding in primer-mcp-eval (0.1.6). Two problems with section updates:

1. `update_ticket(body_sections={"Testable Outcome": ...})` rewrote the body section but left the old `testable_outcome` in the frontmatter, so the ticket contradicted itself. Any section that is also stored in the frontmatter can drift this way.
2. `_update_section` in `tickets.py` joins the new content directly onto the next `\n## ` heading, so the blank line before that heading is lost. The same shows after `complete_task`, where the notes run straight into `## Verification Evidence`.

Make section updates keep the frontmatter counterpart in sync (or refuse or warn when they can't), and keep one blank line before the next heading.

## Testable Outcome
Updating a body section that has a frontmatter counterpart leaves both saying the same thing, and every section update keeps one blank line before the next heading; tests cover both
