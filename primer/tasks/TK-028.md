## What to do
Replace GateErrors for status transitions (e.g. verify before complete, terminal state rejection) with persuasive suggestions. The operation proceeds but the response includes a helpful nudge, not a warning. E.g. "This task hasn't been started yet — you might want to call start_task first so the timeline is accurate." Keep the two-phase concept as guidance the AI drives.
